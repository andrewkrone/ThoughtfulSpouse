Privacy Policy for Thoughtful Spouse Last updated: September 11, 2026 Thoughtful Spouse is designed to be simple and private. This app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. What the app stores The hints you're shown, your chosen notification frequency, your delivery time, and which hints you've marked as completed (along with the date and time) are all stored only on your device, using Apple's standard local storage. None of this information ever leaves your device, and the developer has no access to it. Notifications Thoughtful Spouse uses Apple's local notifications to remind you of a hint on the schedule you choose. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any external server. No third parties This app does not use analytics services, advertising networks, or any third-party SDKs. It does not create a user account, and it does not connect to the internet for any purpose. Children's privacy This app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.
